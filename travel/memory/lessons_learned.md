## Session Log

### Session 1: Initial Planning Setup (2025-02-09)

**What was done:**
- Established baseline itinerary from prior Claude conversation
- Created folder structure for Europe Trip 2025
- Set up budget spreadsheet with all known costs
- Created daily calendar broken into morning/afternoon/evening thirds

**What worked well:**
- Breaking the trip into 3 base cities minimized hotel changes (only 3 check-ins)
- The "thirds" calendar format clearly shows activity density per day
- Keeping activities nature-focused simplified planning (no museum hours, interior schedules)
- Direct-flights-only constraint reduced flight research complexity

**What could be improved:**
- Food budget is not yet detailed - need to research restaurant pricing per city
- Arrabida Natural Park (Day 4) transport needs more research - bus/taxi/rental car options
- Rain contingency plans not yet created for any day
- Specific train times not yet locked in

**Key insights:**
- A family of 4 in Europe for 21 days on $10,000 is achievable but requires discipline on food costs
- The Swiss Travel Pass is the single most important purchase for Week 3
- September shoulder season makes this budget much more feasible than July/August would be
