# Travel Agent - Research Patterns

**Last Updated:** 2025-02-09
**Version:** 1

This document tracks learned research patterns and techniques. Before starting any research task, read this file to apply previously discovered optimizations.

---

## Hotel Research Patterns

### Pattern: Booking Platform Price Comparison
- **Learned**: Hotel direct websites often match Booking.com prices but offer more flexibility
- **Action**: Always check the hotel's own website alongside Booking.com
- **Sources to check**: Hotel website, Booking.com, Hotels.com, Expedia

### Pattern: Family Room Search Terms
- **Learned**: Search terms that yield best results for family accommodation
- **Action**: Use these search queries:
  - "[city] family hotel connecting rooms"
  - "[city] hotel family suite 4 guests"
  - "[city] aparthotel family"
  - "[city] hotel near train station family"

### Pattern: Shoulder Season Pricing
- **Learned**: September in Europe is shoulder season with better prices
- **Action**: When quoting prices, note that September typically offers 20-30% savings vs. peak summer (July-August)

## Flight Research Patterns

### Pattern: Direct Flight Verification
- **Learned**: Not all "direct" flights listed on aggregators are truly nonstop
- **Action**: Always verify on the airline's own website that the flight is nonstop (no technical stops)

### Pattern: Budget Airline Baggage
- **Learned**: Ryanair/easyJet base prices exclude baggage
- **Action**: Always add checked baggage cost to budget airline quotes for fair comparison

---

## Activity Research Patterns

### Pattern: Nature Activity Weather Dependency
- **Learned**: Mountain activities (Switzerland) need weather backup plans
- **Action**: For each mountain day trip, note an alternative lower-elevation activity

### Pattern: Day Trip Timing
- **Learned**: First trains of the day avoid peak crowds at popular destinations
- **Action**: Recommend early departures (8-9 AM) for day trips to Sintra, Montserrat, Lauterbrunnen, etc.

---

## Budget Patterns

### Pattern: Swiss Cost Premium
- **Learned**: Switzerland costs ~2-3x more than Portugal/Spain for food and activities
- **Action**: Budget higher daily food allowance for Switzerland week. Recommend grocery shopping at Migros/Coop

### Pattern: Currency Buffer
- **Learned**: Exchange rate fluctuations can impact budget by 5-10%
- **Action**: Keep 10% buffer on currency-dependent costs

---

## General Patterns

### Pattern: Source Credibility
- **Priority sources**: Official tourism boards, airline websites, hotel websites
- **Secondary sources**: Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Lonely Planet
- **Use with caution**: Blog posts (may be outdated), social media recommendations

### Pattern: Seasonal Availability
- **Learned**: Some routes (especially Air Transat Toronto-Lisbon) are seasonal
- **Action**: Always verify route availability for the specific travel month
