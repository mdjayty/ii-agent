## Trip Overview

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Travelers** | 4 people (family group) |
| **Dates** | September 2025 (3 weeks / 21 days) |
| **Budget** | $10,000 USD total |
| **Departure/Return** | Toronto, Ontario, Canada |
| **Route** | Toronto -> Lisbon -> Porto -> Barcelona -> Zurich -> Toronto |
| **Hotel Check-ins** | 3 total (minimizing hotel changes) |
| **Philosophy** | Slow travel focused on natural beauty, outdoor experiences, and family relaxation |

### Confirmed Preferences
- Nature-focused: beaches, mountains, hiking, coastal walks, waterfalls, scenic viewpoints
- All direct flights only - no layovers
- Minimal hotel changes - base cities with day trips
- Maximum 1 major activity per day
- Relaxed pace - not rushed or over-scheduled
- No museums, no building interiors, no palaces/churches/castles (exteriors OK)

## Week 1: Portugal (Coastal Nature Focus)

### Day 1 - Arrive in Lisbon

#### Morning (8:00 AM - 12:00 PM)
- **Activity**: In-flight (arriving from Toronto)
- **Meal**: Airline meal on flight
- **Transportation**: [TAP Air Portugal](https://www.flytap.com) direct flight, 7h
- **Est. Cost**: Included in flight
- **Notes**: Overnight flight, arrive morning/early afternoon Lisbon time

#### Afternoon (12:00 PM - 5:00 PM)
- **Activity**: Check into [Hotel da Baixa](https://www.hoteldabaixa.com), Rua da Prata, Baixa-Chiado. Rest and settle in
- **Meal**: Light lunch near hotel in Baixa district
- **Transportation**: Metro from Lisbon Airport to Baixa-Chiado station
- **Est. Cost**: $15 (metro + lunch)
- **Notes**: Hotel is 2-min walk to Rossio Station. Family rooms sleep up to 5 (Queen + King beds)

#### Evening (5:00 PM - 10:00 PM)
- **Activity**: Evening walk along Tagus River waterfront, sunset at [Praca do Comercio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pra%C3%A7a_do_Com%C3%A9rcio)
- **Meal**: Dinner at a riverside restaurant in Baixa
- **Transportation**: Walking from hotel
- **Est. Cost**: $20
- **Notes**: Easy introduction to Lisbon, low-energy after travel day

### Day 2 - Sintra Coast Day Trip

#### Morning (8:00 AM - 12:00 PM)

- **Meal**: Breakfast at Hotel da Baixa (included)
- **Transportation**: Train from Rossio Station to Sintra (40 min, EUR 2.30/person)
- **Est. Cost**: $10 (train fare for 4)
- **Notes**: Buy Lisboa Viva card for local transit

#### Afternoon (12:00 PM - 5:00 PM)
- **Activity**: Hike to [Praia da Ursa](https://www.visitportugal.com) - wild beach with massive rock formations
- **Meal**: Packed lunch or cafe near Sintra
- **Transportation**: Local bus between coastal sites
- **Est. Cost**: $20
- **Notes**: Moderate hiking trail to beach. Bring water and sun protection

#### Evening (5:00 PM - 10:00 PM)
- **Activity**: Visit [Praia do Guincho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praia_do_Guincho) - windswept dunes, surfing beach. Return to Lisbon
- **Meal**: Seafood dinner in Sintra or back in Lisbon
- **Transportation**: Train back to Lisbon from Sintra
- **Est. Cost**: $10
- **Notes**: Total day cost ~$40

## Week 3: Switzerland (Alps & Nature Focus)

**IMPORTANT**: Purchase [Swiss Travel Pass](https://www.swisstravelsystem.com) - 8-Day Consecutive
- Cost: ~$380/person x 4 = **$1,520 total**
- Covers: Unlimited trains, buses, boats throughout Switzerland
- 50% discount on mountain railways (Grindelwald First, Harder Kulm, etc.)
- Free boat cruises on lakes

- **Transportation**: Walking within the valley
- **Est. Cost**: $60 (Trummelbach entrance x4)
- **Notes**: Trummelbach Falls are unique - waterfalls carved inside the mountain

#### Evening (5:00 PM - 10:00 PM)
- **Activity**: Walk through alpine meadows, return to Zurich by train
- **Meal**: Dinner on the train or in Zurich
- **Transportation**: Swiss Rail back to Zurich (FREE)

- **Notes**: Total day cost ~$80

- **Meal**: Packed lunch or mountain restaurant
- **Transportation**: Hiking trails
- **Est. Cost**: $0
- **Notes**: Lake Bachalpsee is one of the most photographed alpine lakes

#### Evening (5:00 PM - 10:00 PM)
- **Activity**: Cable car down, train back to Zurich
- **Meal**: Dinner in Zurich
- **Transportation**: Cable car + Swiss Rail (FREE train)
- **Est. Cost**: $0
- **Notes**: Total day cost ~$100
