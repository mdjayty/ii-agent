## Trip Overview

- **Travelers:** 2 people (Portugal & Spain) / 4 people (Switzerland)

- **Departure/Return:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada
- **Route:** Toronto > Lisbon > Porto > Barcelona > Zurich > Interlaken > Zurich > Toronto

- **NO museums, NO building interiors** (exteriors OK)
